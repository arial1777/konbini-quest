Konbini Quest Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-14

This policy explains what information Konbini Quest ("the app") handles. The app is operated by ［operator name］ ("we").

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Quests you cleared and how many times you tried each quiz
  • Foods you marked as "Want" or "Ate", and your ratings
  • Your trip start date, if you entered one
  • Whether you own Travel Pass (a copy kept so locks show correctly when you are offline)

This data is included in your device backup if you use iCloud Backup. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Apple processes Travel Pass purchases. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. The app asks Apple only whether Travel Pass has been purchased on your Apple ID.

Analytics and crash reports

The app does not collect usage analytics or crash reports.

Content updates

The app may download updated text from our server, and app updates from Expo (expo.dev), the service we use to deliver them. These requests include only technical details needed to send the right update, such as the app version, the device platform, and a random ID created for the app on your device. They do not include your name, Apple ID, or anything you saved in the app.

Tracking and advertising

The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not show ads.

Links to other services

Some quests may link to travel services. Those services have their own privacy policies. We may receive a commission if you buy through these links, but we do not share any information about you with them.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above and show the new version in the app.
