Common questions

I bought Travel Pass, but quests are still locked. Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I get a refund? Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the app work without internet? Yes. Everything works offline once the app is installed.

Where is my progress saved? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
